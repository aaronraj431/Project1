More Than a Preschool – A Second Home for Your Little One!

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know before starting your child's preschool journey.

We welcome children starting from 1.5 years for Playgroup, 2.5 years for Nursery, 3.5 years for LKG, and 4.5 years for UKG. Daycare is available for children aged 1.5 to 10 years.
Yes! Our full-day and half-day daycare operates from 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM with freshly cooked nutritious meals, dedicated rest areas, supervised play, and evening homework support.
We maintain a strict 1:8 ratio in Playgroup and 1:10 to 1:12 in Nursery and Kindergarten, supported by dedicated female care attendants in every classroom.
