Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from parents.

What documents are required for admission?

Birth certificate copy, passport-size photographs of the child and parents, and basic ID proof at the time of registration.

Can we visit the campus before finalizing admission?

Yes! Parents are welcome to schedule a guided campus tour during working hours to meet teachers and inspect classrooms.
